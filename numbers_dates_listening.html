🔢 Numbers, Dates, Days, Months & Spelling

A short, targeted drill on the "cheapest" but most easily lost points in IELTS Listening: numbers (13 vs 30), phone numbers, prices, times, days of the week, months, dates, and spelling names letter by letter ("Could you spell that, please?"). These are exactly the categories that show up most in Section 1 (bookings, forms, registrations) — and get lost to carelessness, not lack of English.

Works instantly and for free on your browser's voice. Optionally connect ElevenLabs for a more natural voice, and AI (OpenRouter or a local Claude Server) to add fresh context sentences to the trainer — all via the ⚙ Settings gear below. Keys and addresses are shared with the Listening trainer, so if you've already saved them there, they'll be picked up automatically here too.

Everything below is optional. The trainer works for free on your browser's voice by default. ElevenLabs gives a more natural, "presenter-style" voice; AI (OpenRouter or a local Claude Server) adds new context sentences on top of the built-in ones. Settings are stored in this browser (localStorage) and are shared with the Listening trainer — if you've already saved keys or a Claude Server address there, they'll be picked up here automatically.

2 · AI Source: More Sentence Variety

The AI never invents the numbers/dates themselves — the trainer's deterministic generator does that, so a correct answer is always guaranteed correct. AI is only used to write more natural context sentences (into which the generator inserts the number/date/time) — this widens the pool of phrases that sound more realistic out loud.

How to get an OpenRouter API key
  1. Go to openrouter.ai/keys and sign in (Google, GitHub, or email).
  2. Click Create Key, give it a name (e.g. "IELTS trainer"), and copy the key — it starts with sk-or-v1-....
  3. Top up your balance at openrouter.ai/credits, or pick a model with the :free suffix.
  4. Paste the key below and click "Save".
